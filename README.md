# 🏅 Maths Sports Day

A phone-friendly maths revision game for **Cambridge Lower Secondary Years 7–9** (classes **J1T, J2T, J3T**).
Students race, jump and relay through questions on their **own phone**. There's nothing to install and no login.

**▶ Play:** open the GitHub Pages link for this repository (Settings → Pages shows it), or scan the QR code in the game’s 🧑‍🏫 **Teacher** corner.

---

## The events

| Event | What happens | Medals |
|---|---|---|
| ⚡ **100 m Sprint** | 10 quick questions, as fast as you can. A wrong answer adds 3 s. | 🥇 ≤ 60 s · 🥈 ≤ 90 s · 🥉 ≤ 130 s |
| 🚧 **Hurdles** | Choose one Learner’s Book unit and clear 10 hurdles: 3 Bronze → 4 Silver → 3 Gold. Hints and worked solutions are included. | 🥇 9+ · 🥈 7+ · 🥉 5+ correct |
| 🏁 **4 × 100 m Relay** | Mixed revision in 4 legs (Number → Algebra → Geometry & measure → Statistics & probability), 3 questions per leg. A wrong answer adds 10 s. | 🥇 11+ · 🥈 9+ · 🥉 7+ correct |
| 🏃 **Marathon** | 1 km per correct answer, 3 lives, with a 💧 water station (+1 life) every 7 km. Can you reach 42 km? | 🥇 42 km · 🥈 21 km · 🥉 10 km |
| 🎟️ **Class Challenge** | Type your teacher’s code (e.g. `8R-TIGER42`). Everyone gets **exactly the same questions**, so results are fair to compare. | Result card to show the teacher |

Every wrong answer shows the correct answer and a **worked solution**. At the end of every race there is a full review.
Students earn XP and level up: Rookie → Club Runner → County Athlete → National Star → Champion → Legend.

## For teachers

The Teacher corner is **hidden from students**. Open it with your private link, which is the game link with `#teacher` added to the end, then enter your teacher PIN. After that, a **🧑‍🏫 Teacher** button appears on the home screen of that device only. You can lock it again from inside the Teacher corner.

1. **Share.** Project the QR code (there’s a full-screen button). Students scan it with their camera.
2. **Class Challenge.** Choose a year and an event, then tap *Create code*. Codes look like `7S-MANGO27` (year, then **S**print / **R**elay / **M**arathon). You can also invent your own, e.g. `9R-FRIDAY`.
3. **Question preview.** See a fresh example of every question type for each year, with answers and worked solutions. Use it to check the content matches what you have taught.

### Lesson ideas
- **Warm-up (5 min):** ⚡ Sprint. Ask who beat their personal best.
- **After teaching a unit (10–15 min):** 🚧 Hurdles on that unit.
- **Exam revision (15–20 min):** 🏁 Relay covers all four strands.
- **Class competition:** create a Challenge code, give 10 minutes, and collect screenshots of the result cards.
- **Homework:** “Reach the half-marathon (21 km) before Friday.”
- **Get moving:** 5 star jumps between Relay legs, or run it outdoors with phones at a “station” table.

### Wellbeing by design
Medals reward accuracy, so every student can win gold. Personal bests matter more than beating others. Scores stay **on each phone**: there are no accounts, no public leaderboards and no data collection.

## What’s covered

Questions are **generated fresh every time**, so the numbers never repeat in the same way. Each year uses its own Learner’s Book (Byrd, Byrd & Pearce, 2nd edition) units, and each unit is tiered 🥉 Bronze (warm-up) → 🥈 Silver (core) → 🥇 Gold (challenge). Everything is non-calculator; circle questions use π = 3.14. Year 8 Unit 6 (Planning and collecting data) is not included.

### Updating
Edit files directly on GitHub (click a file, then ✏️). After changing anything, open `sw.js` and change `maths-sports-day-v1` to `-v2` (then `-v3` next time, and so on) so students’ phones fetch the new version.

## Publishing on GitHub Pages
1. Create a **public** repository (e.g. `maths-sports-day`) and upload all these files to it.
2. Go to **Settings → Pages**. Under *Build and deployment*, choose **Deploy from a branch**, select branch **main** and folder **/ (root)**, then click **Save**.
3. After about a minute, the game is live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/maths-sports-day/`.
